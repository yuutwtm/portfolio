About
소개

I believe in the accountability of us adults in making our students ask better questions; in convincing them that some criticisms are indeed constructive; in providing them the adequate introduction into the public sphere; in, ultimately, engaging them with the world that will be theirs to protect.

My methods include, but are not limited to, critical reasoning, essay writing, designing presentations, all the while embracing technological advancements relevant to our end goal.

학생들이 더 좋은 질문을 던지게 하는 것, 어떤 비판은 정말로 도움이 된다는 걸 납득시키는 것, 공론장에 처음 발을 들이는 길을 제대로 안내해 주는 것. 그래서 결국엔 언젠가 자기가 지켜야 할 세상과 마주 서게 하는 것. 저는 이게 다 어른이 져야 할 몫이라고 생각해요.

주로 쓰는 방법은 비판적으로 생각하기, 에세이 쓰기, 발표 만들기예요. 물론 여기에만 매달리지는 않고요. 목표에 도움이 된다면 새로운 기술도 얼마든지 가져다 씁니다.

Skills & Expertise
관심분야

Philosophy is fundamentally the rigorous practice of questioning and being questioned. Can you ask the right questions? Can you effectively defend your points? How will you react when your world is challenged? True critical thinking demands nothing less than genuine openness to being wrong.

  • Critical reading
  • Media literacy education
  • Facilitating formal discussions (BP Debate, Model United Nations) and non-formal discussions

Messages matter. Delivery matters too. I make extensive use of both textual and visual language.

  • Essay writing
  • Student-directed projects
  • Contents production through graphic tools (Adobe, Microsoft Powerpoint, Figma, Nano Banana)
  • Contents production through AI tools (Vibe coding using Claude and Gemini)
